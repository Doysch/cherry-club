Grown-ups' corner

Each square is one fact. Colour shows how secure it is: from new learning getting there nearly fluent to fluent. A fact becomes fluent when answered correctly, without a hint, within 6 seconds (the Multiplication Tables Check standard). Wrong answers move a fact back a step and bring it around again sooner.

Settings

How this game teaches

  • Derived-fact strategies, with the working shown: ×3 is double plus one more, ×4 is double-double, ×6 is five lots plus one, ×7 is five lots plus two lots, ×12 is ten lots plus two lots. This builds the habit of showing working that her report asks for.
  • Area model: every fact can be seen as a rectangle of squares, linking tables to area.
  • Spaced retrieval: facts are scheduled to come back after growing gaps; wobbly facts return sooner.
  • Applying, not just recalling: secure facts return as division, missing numbers, area, durations in minutes, days and weeks, months and years, dozens, litres, money and equivalent fractions.
  • Mistakes are teaching moments: a wrong answer shows the trick, offers one more go, and the fact returns later in the same session.
  • Speed is a choice: quests are untimed; Cherry Dash is a 60-second challenge with a personal best.
